# Small project examples:

### Statistics and Hypothesis Testing:
**Benefit:** When we have a sample of subjects, we use inferential statistics 
to infer about relationships in the true population.  

**Probability Density Function:** Easiest to visualize on a normal curve.  
Represents the probability density of values based on the observed data, 
can be used to get a "probability" of obtaining a value within a given 
range based on the data you observed.  
- Ends up being the area under the normal curve for the range of values 
you specified.  

**Cumulative Density Function:** The PDF represented cumulatively across 
values, so you can get the probability of seeing a value or lower (or 
higher).  

**Example problem:** You developed an injury screening form where higher 
scores indicate greater possible risk factors for future injury. You 
collected data on 100 patients in a clinic. You want to know, if rolling 
this out to all your clinics, what is the probability of a patient 
having a score between 30 and 50 on the form, and what is the probability 
of having between 40 and 50 on the form?  
- Probability of 30-50 is 0.32
- Probability of 40-50 is 0.10
    - If wanting to use in clinics for screening purposes, a higher 
    threshold of 40 on the scale gives a reasonable proportion of patients 
    that can be feasibly routed for intervention or tracked for outcomes.
